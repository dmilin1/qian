## Placement of curated everyday words

Corpus frequency badly understates the sweep and audit additions in Taiwanese family speech. Subtitles write numbers as digits, and words like 好吃, 便宜 and 台灣 fall in the mainland corpora's blind spots. At their blended rank they would sit at the very end of the list, after 權利 or 監獄. ## Kept despite a junk flag

- 們 (106; boundMorpheme): Kept although a bound suffix: the plural marker (我們, 你們, 朋友們) is a core grammar item.
- 喔 (990; interjection): Taiwan substitute for 哦 (slot of 哦, blend rank 138): Taiwan writes the 'oh / OK' interjection and friendly sentence-final particle as 喔; merges 哦 and 噢.
- 美國 (168; properNoun:place): Place name kept: extremely common in conversation.
- 很多 (229; fragment, noDict): Kept although not a dictionary headword: 很多 is the normal spoken way to say 'many/a lot' (多 alone is rarely attributive).
- 中國 (326; properNoun:place): Place name kept: extremely common in conversation.
- 台灣 (4157; properNoun:place): Sanity-sweep addition (blend rank 4157): the learner's language community; ranks low only because both corpora are mainland-heavy. Spelled 台灣 (not 臺灣): 台灣 is the everyday form (MOE standard 臺灣).
- 日本 (562; properNoun:place): Place name kept: extremely common in Taiwanese conversation (travel, food, products).
