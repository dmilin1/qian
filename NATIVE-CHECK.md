# Native-speaker check

Thank you for helping! qian teaches the 1000 most common Mandarin words **by ear**, with Taiwan pronunciation. The recordings come from a computer voice, and the pronunciations follow the Ministry of Education dictionary (教育部國語辭典). The app was checked automatically, but these items need a real Taiwanese ear. The most important ones come first. Even the first ten help a lot.

## How to check a word

1. Open **https://dmilin1.github.io/qian/** and go to the **Words** tab.
2. Type the number shown after each item into the search box, for example `#40`. You can also type the characters or pinyin.
3. Tap the word. On its page you can play the word (normal and slow) and its example sentence.
4. If something is wrong, tap **Flag a problem**. Pick a reason ("Audio sounds wrong", "Wrong pinyin or tones", …) and write what you actually say in the note, for example "I say hàn".
5. When you are done, go to **Stats → Flagged cards → Export flags (CSV)** and send the file to Dimitrie.

If something is fine, you don't need to do anything.
